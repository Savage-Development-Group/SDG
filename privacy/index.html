Privacy policy.

Last updated October 4, 2026

Information you provide

When you submit the project inquiry form, Savage Dev Group receives the name, email address, project type, and message you provide. This information is used to review and respond to your inquiry and, when appropriate, manage the resulting business relationship.

Site and interaction data

The website may record basic interaction data such as page views, referring page, requested path, and form-submission events. This information is used to understand site performance, improve the experience, and follow up on inquiries. Form information is transmitted to the services used to deliver and manage project inquiries.

Service providers

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. FormSubmit is used to deliver inquiry emails. Task Forge services may be used to record site events and manage inquiry workflows. Those providers process data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Retention and sharing

Inquiry information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for communication, business records, security, and legal obligations. SDG does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the site, business, or users.

Your choices

You may request access to or deletion of information associated with an inquiry by contacting SDG through the contact form. Certain records may be retained when required for legal, accounting, security, or contractual reasons.

Updates

This policy may change as the website and its services evolve. The current revision date appears at the top of this page.
