Independent development studio

Small by design. Serious about the work.

Savage Dev Group combines product thinking, design, and development to create focused websites and software without unnecessary layers.

Clarity before complexity

Digital projects get expensive when goals remain vague, decisions are disconnected, and complexity is treated as progress. SDG starts by making the problem, audience, constraints, and desired outcome clear.

That understanding guides everything that follows—from page structure and interface hierarchy to data models and launch priorities.

One connected practice

Strategy, content structure, interface design, and implementation are treated as parts of the same product decision. This keeps the original goal present through the details and shortens the distance between an idea and a working release.

Principles behind the work

  • Useful beats impressive. A product should make something important easier.
  • Simple has to be earned. Clear interfaces come from understanding complexity, not hiding it.
  • Ship a strong first version. Real use produces better information than endless speculation.
  • Build for the next decision. The foundation should support growth without pretending every future need is known.

Owned products sharpen client work

Building and operating Task Forge OS keeps SDG close to the full product lifecycle: positioning, user workflows, application design, infrastructure, billing, analytics, support, and iteration. Those lessons carry directly into client engagements.

Marketing websites, web applications, customer portals, internal tools, e-commerce experiences, integrations, and product strategy.
